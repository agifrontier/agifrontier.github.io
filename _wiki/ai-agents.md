## 关键问题

- **What makes a system an agent?** An agent combines model reasoning with tools, state, goals, and feedback loops rather than returning one isolated answer.
- **Why track agents separately?** Agent systems expose reliability, evaluation, security, and product workflow issues that simple chat interfaces do not show.
