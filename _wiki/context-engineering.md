## 关键问题

- **How is context engineering different from prompt engineering?** Prompt engineering focuses on instructions, while context engineering designs the whole information environment around the model.
- **Why is it important for agents?** Agents depend on long-running state, tools, traces, and memory, so context quality directly affects reliability.
